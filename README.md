## Why diagnosis matters

A campaign can have plenty of clicks and still produce poor business results. In other cases, the problem may be the landing page, offer, audience, creative, or tracking setup.

Instead of changing everything at once, start by identifying where the campaign may be losing performance.

A useful process is:

**Observe → Diagnose → Form a hypothesis → Test → Measure → Improve**

## Ad Performance Diagnosis Framework

A simple way to investigate campaign performance is to follow the customer journey:

**Impression → Click → Landing Page → Engagement → Conversion**

When performance drops, look for the stage where the biggest change is happening.

For example:

- Low CTR may indicate a relevance, messaging, creative, or intent issue.
- Good CTR with weak conversions may point to the landing page or offer.
- Rising CPC can indicate changes in competition or traffic mix.
- Rising CPA may be caused by higher traffic costs, weaker conversion rates, or both.
- Unexpected conversion changes should always be checked against tracking data.
